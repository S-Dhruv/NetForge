## Benchmark Strategy
A small, fixed evaluation set to evaluate research quality, citation integrity, and failure handling over time.

### Evaluation Criteria
1. **Relevance:** Does the briefing/report directly address the question/topic?
2. **Novelty:** Measures duplicate rate across repeated scheduled runs.
3. **Citation Support:** Verified alignment between claims and cited source passages.
4. **Calibration:** Accurate labeling of supported, conflicting, and uncertain claims.
5. **Cost & Latency:** Tracking token consumption, request latency, and failure rates.

15-16th Nov '26 - Added a `free` channel (a queue used to send signals) so a worker can tell the pool when it has finished handling a job and its slot is available. `signalFree` returns true when that signal is sent, or when no channel was provided; it does not check whether the job reached a terminal state. If the worker received a nil or non-`RUNNING` job, it reports the invalid input and signals that its slot is available without running the handler. During shutdown, context cancellation tells the pool to stop claiming jobs and gives handlers a chance to stop. A handler must cooperate with its context; after it returns, the worker saves the outcome and tries to signal the pool. If the `select` chooses `ctx.Done()`, `signalFree` returns false and that worker exits.
